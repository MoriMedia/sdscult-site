Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026

SDS Cult is operated by SDS Threads LLC. This page explains what we collect on sdscult.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter and the time you signed up. That is all we ask for.

How we use it

We use your email only to tell you about SDS Cult drops and launches. We do not sell, rent or trade your email address.

Who helps us run the site

  • Waitlist signups are stored with Google (Google Forms and Google Sheets).
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like most web hosts, these services receive your IP address when your browser loads the page.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics on this site, and the site does not set cookies of its own.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time, and you can ask us to delete your email address or tell you what we hold about you. Email hello@sdscult.com and we will take care of it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
